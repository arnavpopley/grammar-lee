# Targeted grammar cases: the kind of mistakes made when typing emails, messages and applications fast.
# Format: wrong sentence || corrected sentence.  "## name" starts a category.
# Scoring: caught = an underline overlaps the part that changed; fixed = applying the top suggestions
# gives exactly the corrected sentence; false alarm = any underline on the corrected sentence.
# Corrected sentences are British English with Oxford commas.

## its / it's
Its been a long week for the team. || It's been a long week for the team.
I think its worth applying early. || I think it's worth applying early.
The company changed it's pricing last year. || The company changed its pricing last year.
Every society has it's own budget. || Every society has its own budget.
Maybe its fine as it is. || Maybe it's fine as it is.

## your / you're
Your going to love the new dashboard. || You're going to love the new dashboard.
Let me know if your free on Friday. || Let me know if you're free on Friday.
Thanks for you're help with the application. || Thanks for your help with the application.
Thanks for you time. || Thanks for your time.

## their / there / they're
Their going to announce the results tomorrow. || They're going to announce the results tomorrow.
I left my laptop over their. || I left my laptop over there.
The founders presented there roadmap to investors. || The founders presented their roadmap to investors.
Their is a problem with the server. || There is a problem with the server.

## then / than
The model is faster then the baseline. || The model is faster than the baseline.
We had more applicants this year then last year. || We had more applicants this year than last year.
I would rather call then email. || I would rather call than email.

## could of / should of / would of
I could of finished it yesterday. || I could have finished it yesterday.
We should of asked them earlier. || We should have asked them earlier.
It would of been better to wait. || It would have been better to wait.

## a / an
It was an useful exercise. || It was a useful exercise.
She gave a honest answer. || She gave an honest answer.
We need a update on the budget. || We need an update on the budget.
This is an unique opportunity. || This is a unique opportunity.
It took a hour to load. || It took an hour to load.

## repeated words
I wanted to follow up on the the proposal. || I wanted to follow up on the proposal.
Please send it to to me by Friday. || Please send it to me by Friday.
We will will review it next week. || We will review it next week.
